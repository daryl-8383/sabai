FAQ

Fair questions.

Is there any nicotine in this at all?

None. Zero. SABAI contains only herbal ingredients — menthol, camphor, borneol, eucalyptus, and peppermint oils. There is nothing addictive in the tube.

Will this make me quit nicotine?

SABAI isn't a cessation drug and we won't pretend it is. What it does is give your hands and lungs the ritual they're used to — the reach, the pull, the exhale — with nothing addictive attached. Many people find that replacing the ritual is the hardest part of cutting back. That's the part we built for.

Can I use it on a plane / at work / anywhere?

Yes. There's no vapor, no smoke, no smell beyond a faint mint you'd have to be very close to notice. It's a solid herbal inhaler — TSA treats it like a lip balm.

How long does a tube last?

With regular use, about 2–3 months per tube. When the scent fades, it's done — recap it and recycle.

Is it safe?

Ya dom has been used daily across Thailand for generations. Use it as directed — inhale near the nostril, don't ingest it, and keep it away from kids and pets. If you're pregnant or have a respiratory condition, check with your doctor first.
